# DigitalHut Preliminary Name Scan

Date: June 18, 2026

This is a preliminary operational scan, not a legal opinion or trademark clearance.

## Confirmed current use

- `digitalhut.app`
  - DigitalHut Observatory.
  - 3D observatory, markets, wallets, GLB assets, environmental and planetary presentation.

## Similar public name found

- `digital-hut.com`
  - The Digital Hut Zambia.
  - Publicly describes itself as a digital marketing agency offering website design and development, branding, photography, email, and related digital services.

## Risk interpretation

- The names are highly similar in appearance and sound.
- The services overlap at least partly in website development and digital services.
- Country, customer market, actual use dates, trademark registrations, and service classifications affect the legal analysis.
- Copyright protects original code, artwork, text, Guardian characters, and specific visual expression. It generally does not reserve a business name or the general idea of sliding controls, an observatory, or an AI presentation system.
- Brand-name protection is primarily a trademark issue.

## Recommended next actions

1. Run a comprehensive USPTO search for:
   - `DIGITALHUT`
   - `DIGITAL HUT`
   - `DIGITAL-HUT`
   - phonetic and similar marks
2. Search state business registries, app stores, domain records, social platforms, and common-law web use.
3. Ask a U.S. trademark attorney to review likelihood of confusion and filing classes.
4. Preserve evidence of first use:
   - dated deployments
   - source commits
   - screenshots
   - invoices
   - marketing
   - domain registration
5. Consider filing the word mark and, separately, an original logo/Guardian design if counsel clears them.

USPTO advises searching for similar trademarks and conducting a comprehensive clearance search, not only exact matches.
